## Findings

Method note: 12 Exa searches (3 per sub-question, each with one 2025/2026-recency query) and 6 deep reads. No search errored. Trade press (BoF, Vogue/Vogue Business, Modern Retail, Fortune, Hypebeast, Complex) surfaced for the drop model and Supreme/Corteiz; the end-to-end launch playbooks came almost entirely from agency and practitioner sites, not BoF/Shopify/Klaviyo. Klaviyo's own Academy guide and 2026 benchmarks (cited via a secondary page) are the closest platform-official sources.

### 1. Collection-launch playbooks: phases, sequencing, assets, gates

- **Eight-week template (agency).** Week -8/-7 strategy and asset calendar; Week -6/-5 content production (hero campaign imagery, product detail shots, lookbook, vertical social formats, press-quality editorial); Week -4 teaser phase starts plus influencer seeding (tier-one 3-4 weeks out, micro 2-3 weeks out, press 4 weeks out); Week -3 hero-piece reveal email and VIP early-access waitlist (24-48h before public); Week -2 full reveal: "Complete lookbook publication (digital and PDF)", campaign video premiere, press release, collection page live with products "coming soon"; Week -1 countdown emails at Day 7/3/1 and a final QA checklist (product pages, pricing, inventory, mobile, checkout, confirmation templates). Launch week email cadence Day 1 launch, Day 2 bestsellers, Day 3 style guide, Day 5 social proof, Day 7 last chance. Paid budget split suggested: Instagram/Facebook 50-60%, Google 20-25%, TikTok 10-15%, Pinterest 5-10%. [BS Agency](https://bs-agency.co.uk/fashion-collection-launch-marketing-8-week-strategy-template/) (2026-03-12). Author's view, not data: successful launches hit "sell-through rates exceeding 70% in the first week".
- **Seven-phase checklist with explicit go/no-go gate.** Phases: commercial strategy, offer/operations, content production, ecommerce readiness, pre-launch demand, launch execution, post-launch. Step 30 is a formal go/no-go: confirm inventory, site status, pricing, tracking, links, email, paid, organic, customer service, fulfillment; "If a critical system is not ready, decide whether to delay, reduce scope or launch selectively." Asset list: campaign hero imagery, product-page photography, on-model fit, detail views, vertical content, paid variations, email assets, website banners, PR imagery. Recommends 8-12 weeks of planning built around the longest dependency. [Phil Halfmann](https://philhalfmann.com/fashion-product-launch-checklist/) (2026-08-06).
- **12-week campaign timeline (same author).** Production should complete about five weeks before launch to leave time for selects, retouching, page builds, media setup and QA; Week 3 = full customer-path QA and analytics validation before spend; Week 2 = public anticipation; final week = change freeze. Six-week compressed variant provided. KPI ladder: attention (reach, video completion, share of search), intent (waitlist conversion, email engagement, add-to-cart), commercial (CVR, units, revenue, contribution margin, CAC, ROAS), customer quality (new-customer rate, repeat, returns). [Phil Halfmann](https://philhalfmann.com/product-launch-campaign-timeline/) (2026-08-07).
- **Drop-style three-phase model (agency).** Pre-drop weeks -4 to -1, launch window 24-72 hours, post-drop day 3 onward. Klaviyo pre-drop sequence: Week 4 story with no product photos, Week 3 first teaser, Week 2 behind-the-scenes, Week 1 countdown with VIP early access (1-2 hours). Meta: 30-40% of budget on awareness pre-drop, 60-70% on conversion in first 48h; turn conversion campaigns off pre-drop. Seed 3-6 micro-influencers 2-3 weeks out so content lands 5-10 days before launch. Launch-day tech gate: "minimum 6-8 product images, at least one on model", real-time stock messaging, checkout tested on three phones, back-in-stock flow live, no launch-day discount. Post-drop: hold full price 2-4 weeks, private last-chance to waitlist non-converters, archive at -10 to -15% after 4-6 weeks. Author's claim (SINGLE-SOURCE, agency experience): drops with 4-week pre-drop generate "3-5x more revenue in the first 48 hours" vs a standard new-arrival launch. [Landing Partners](https://www.landing.partners/blog/fashion-drop-marketing-collection-launch-strategy) (undated, "2026" in title).
- **Five-email launch sequence with T-minus timing.** Teaser/waitlist T-10 to T-14, early access T-1 to T-2, launch T-0 morning, social proof T+1 to T+3, last call T+5 to T+7; segmentation rules (waitlist, VIP, engaged, purchasers suppressed). Cites Klaviyo: three-email sequences $24.9M vs $3.8M single emails; flows are ~2% of volume and ~37% of email revenue; and Waitlister: waitlist pages convert ~11% of visitors vs 6.6% landing-page median. [Talk Shop](https://www.letstalkshop.com/blog/shopify-product-launch-email-sequence) (2026-06-25).
- **Klaviyo Academy (platform-official).** Three stages: pre-launch (announcement to all, SMS teaser, email teaser to engaged, VIP early access via SMS then email, VIP last-chance), launch (midnight email to engaged/VIP non-purchasers, daytime email+SMS to all), post-launch. SMS reserved for exclusive or time-sensitive messages. [Klaviyo Academy](https://academy.klaviyo.com/en-us/quick-guides/promote-a-new-product-with-omnichannel-marketing) (undated).
- **Klaviyo 2026 benchmarks (via secondary).** Flows produce ~41% of email revenue from 5.3% of sends; clothing/accessories flow placed-order rate 2.15% vs 0.12% for campaigns. Recommends sending drop campaigns to a dedicated drop landing page with story, lookbook and full lineup rather than a filtered collection. [Fudge.ai](https://www.fudge.ai/guides/klaviyo-flows-fashion/) (2026-08-27), citing klaviyo.com benchmarks.
- **Other cadence data points.** Three-email drop flow (T-3 teaser, drop day with VIP 2-4h early, T+2 selling email); claims email+SMS beats email-only by 15-25% for drops (SINGLE-SOURCE, unsourced). [GOSH Digital](https://www.goshdigital.co/blog/email-marketing-for-fashion-brands) (2025-07-29). Expect 10-14 touches over 10-14 days across teaser/waitlist/launch/abandon/post-purchase. [Deni Copywriter](https://denicopywriter.com/klaviyo-email-flow-templates-apparel-launch/) (2025-12-13). 5-8 emails, start 1-3 weeks out. [Fabrikn](https://www.fabrikn.com/blog/fashion-brand-launch-email-sequence/) (2026-03-30). T-21 moodboard, T-14 name/date, T-7 BTS, T-3 lookbook + pricing, launch-day livestream/email; vendor claims 30-40% of email-list growth occurs during capsule pre-launch (SINGLE-SOURCE). [Shanghai Garment](https://shanghaigarment.com/how-to-turn-a-capsule-drop-into-a-marketing-event/) (undated).
- **90-day first-collection timeline.** Scoped to 3-6 styles; influencer outreach 4-6 weeks before launch; Week 12 = QC, photography, go/no-go on launch date; Day 90 go-live. [Plucky Reach](https://pluckyreach.com/blog/90-day-fashion-brand-launch-timeline) (2026-03-13). Fabrikn's 90-day pre-launch splits Days 90-61 setup, 60-31 audience building, 30-1 anticipation. Example goals: 2,000 subscribers, 5% waitlist conversion, 10 creator collabs; roadmap example: sell 70% of first run in 60 days, 500 signups, 2.5% landing CVR. [Fabrikn](https://www.fabrikn.com/blog/fashion-brand-pre-launch-marketing-plan/) (2026-03-29), [Fabrikn](https://www.fabrikn.com/blog/fashion-brand-product-launch-roadmap/) (2026-03-30).
- **Launch orchestration failure mode.** Email and SMS should fire off one trigger within the same minute; independently scheduled sends drift 5-20 min and customers hit unpropagated pages; sold-out fallback should swap CTAs to restock waitlist. Worked example (600-unit capsule, 1,200 waitlist, 180 VIPs) is illustrative, not a case study. [US Tech Automations](https://ustechautomations.com/resources/blog/automate-dtc-product-launch-klaviyo-postscript-2026) (2026-06-18).

### 3. Website relaunch + collection launch together; imagery; KPIs and benchmarks

- **Live example (Sept 2026).** AllSaints released Aaron Esh's debut AW26 collection with a campaign and a rebuilt "content-driven" site (AllSaints World) combining campaign imagery, lookbooks, articles and profiles, plus a free zine during LFW; Esh: "This is a new chapter of our story where we live up to our history." [FashionNetwork](https://in.fashionnetwork.com/news/Allsaints-starts-new-chapter-with-first-aaron-esh-collection,revamped-website,1862716.html) (2026-09-02).
- **Pre-order-model rebuild case.** Frahm (menswear jackets): unclear pre-order messaging drove hesitation; agency fixed messaging on PDP, cart and checkout first, then rebuilt after ~10 months; post-rebuild behaviour took ~45 days to stabilise (paid and SEO were paused); reported +61% revenue growth, +80% sales, +20% AOV (agency-reported). [WIRO](https://www.wiro.agency/blog/how-wiro-helped-frahm-turn-pre-orders-into-a-scalable-growth-engine) (2026-08-05).
- **Relaunch as migration.** Baseline 12 months of GSC/analytics; full URL map with 301s; rollback available 24-72h and data kept 90 days; escalation triggers (10%+ drop in indexed pages, 404 surge, CWV regression); daily checks week 1, weekly for 90 days; judge by business outcomes vs baseline, not Lighthouse scores. [Refact](https://refact.co/insights/migration/website-relaunch-playbook) (2026-08-24). Don't rewrite top-5 ranking pages during migration; rankings recover 2-4 weeks, full stabilisation 3-6 months; forgotten `noindex` is the most common deindexation cause. [Zenku](https://zenku.studio/en/blog/website-relaunch-checklist-2026/) (2026-06-02). Go live outside peak season, communicate to existing customers in advance, prepare password resets, migrate order history, gradual traffic ramp, keep old environment as rollback. [Clickpuls](https://clickpuls.com/en-AT/knowledge/shop-relaunch-checklist) (2025-12-02).
- **Shopify (platform-official) on announcing a relaunch.** Start announcement strategy "a few weeks in advance" with teasers on site, social and email; Belstaff relaunch case. [Shopify](https://www.shopify.com/ca/blog/website-relaunch) (2024-12-30).
- **Imagery gate for pre-orders.** "Real photos should carry the burden of proof"; red flags include garment shown only in styled scenes, no seam/texture close-ups, and inconsistent fabric across images; cites FTC Mail Order Rule 30-day shipping basis and Google Merchant Center AI-image metadata requirement. [Gorgeautiful](https://www.gorgeautiful.com/ai-lookbook-images-vs-real-photos-fashion-preorders/) (2026-05-29). Regulatory framing: EU AI Act Article 50 machine-readable marking of AI images, FTC Section 5, UK ASA on undisclosed AI; ecommerce return rates ~19-20.5% with expectation gaps a top driver; recommends C2PA provenance and "digital sample" labelling. Vendor-authored. [Style3D](https://www.style3d.com/blog/truth-in-advertising-for-ai-product-renders-for-fashion-teams/) (2026-06-24). Hybrid workflow (real capture, AI post) recommended; "As long as the imagery matches what arrives in the box" is the trust condition. Vendor-authored. [Rewarx](https://www.rewarx.com/blogs/4-minute-test-ai-product-photos-shoppers) (2026-06-15). Broken Planet's disclosed AI campaign split its community (see §2).
- **Benchmarks: site conversion.** Centra (500 fashion brands): median 2.4%; women's 3.6%, men's 0.8%, sportswear 2.8%, footwear 2.2%, accessories 7.4%, luxury lower. [Centra](https://centra.com/news/conversion-rate-fashion-ecommerce-benchmarks) (2025-02-28). Agency sample of 19 DTC stores (Jul 2025-Jun 2026): median 1.17%, IQR 0.92-1.52%. [Top Growth Marketing](https://topgrowthmarketing.com/dtc-benchmarks-2026/) (undated, "July 2026"). Fashion 78% mobile traffic / 47% mobile purchases; mobile CVR 1.2% vs desktop 1.9% (Envive via Foundry). [Foundry CRO](https://foundrycro.com/blog/dtc-fashion-marketing-benchmarks-2026/) (2026-05-12).
- **Benchmarks: paid.** Meta fashion median ROAS 2.18x, top decile 6.0x (AdAmigo); Google fashion median 4.07x; DTC fashion CAC $37-120, most $45-75; repeat purchase 15-17%. [Foundry CRO](https://foundrycro.com/blog/dtc-fashion-marketing-benchmarks-2026/) (2026-05-12). Agency sample: Meta ROAS 2.70, Google 4.07; break-even ROAS = 1 ÷ gross margin. [Top Growth Marketing](https://topgrowthmarketing.com/dtc-benchmarks-2026/). Q1 2023-Q4 2024 cohort: Meta ROAS 3.6x, email open 22.4%, email CTR 2.6%, landing CVR 3.8%. [Benchmarketing](https://www.benchmarketing.org/benchmarks/industries/fashion-apparel) (undated).
- **Benchmarks: unit economics.** $10.1B-sales dataset: median CAC $54, LTV:CAC 2.4x, 90-day repurchase 14.7% for new customers; Fashion & Apparel contribution margin 60.5% with marketing 22.1% of revenue; health bands (90-day repurchase >25% elite, <10% critical). [Lifetimely / AMP](https://useamp.com/blog/2025-dtc-mega-report/) (2026-08-26).
- **Benchmarks: email/SMS and list.** Klaviyo SMS "Great" bands: click ≥14.6%, conversion ≥2.1%, unsub <0.5%; apparel site CVR 1.9-3.6%, repeat ~25-26%. [Attribuly](https://blog2.attribuly.com/fashion-industry-benchmarks-2025-best-practices/) (2025-09-25). Agency targets: list growth 5-10% MoM, campaign open 25-35%, flow share 40-55% of email revenue. [GOSH Digital](https://www.goshdigital.co/blog/email-marketing-for-fashion-brands) (2025-07-29). Waitlist signup ~11% of visitors (Waitlister via Talk Shop, above). No authoritative waitlist-to-purchase benchmark found; Fabrikn's "5% of waitlist" is an example goal, not data.
- **Recommended launch KPIs.** Sell-through, revenue, CVR, AOV, return rate, email subscriber growth, social engagement. [Fabrikn](https://www.fabrikn.com/blog/fashion-brand-product-launch-roadmap/) (2026-03-30). Four-tier ladder in Halfmann (above). Lookbook-specific: downloads/views, email CTR, buyer inquiries, press pickups, linked sales. [Fabrikn](https://www.fabrikn.com/blog/how-to-create-a-fashion-brand-lookbook/) (2026-03-29).

### 4. Lookbook production workflow

- **Shot list as contract.** Look matrix per look/SKU with 8 baseline frames: straight-on full length, three-quarter, back, mid-length styling, detail, movement, horizontal with text space, vertical for stories/Reels. Mark frames required/useful/experimental and finish required first. Product-accuracy checklist (steam, closures, colour references, note sample defects) and a written retouching agreement so retouching "should not silently change the product being sold." Plan crops per channel (PDP/grid, homepage hero, email header, feed/story/Reel, paid, wholesale PDF, press). [Signal Studio NYC](https://signalstudionyc.com/blog/fashion-lookbook-shot-list) (2026-08-20).
- **Lookbook vs campaign.** Lookbook = 12-20 pages, product-forward, SKU/colourway/price captions, $3k-15k, 2-5 production days; campaign = 3-6 hero images, mood-forward, $10k-60k, 3-10 days; build the lookbook first if capital is limited; edit to the strongest 70%; refresh within 90 days of a new capsule. Author claims "82% of press editors still request a PDF" and a McKinsey 2025 stat, neither linkable (SINGLE-SOURCE, unverified). [Vistoya](https://vistoya.com/publication/how-to-create-fashion-lookbook-buyers-press-independent-designers-2026) (2026-04-15).
- **Lookbook direction lead times and costs.** 2-4 weeks lean, 4-6 weeks with styling boards, 6-8 weeks with casting/location/multi-channel; direction $1.5k-4k (up to $5k-10k full), basic studio shoot $2.5k-6k plus talent; minimum shot list: front full-body per look, back for key silhouettes, detail crop, one or two editorial frames; sequence hero look, then range, then supporting looks. [Fabrikn](https://www.fabrikn.com/blog/lookbook-direction-service-for-clothing-launches/) (2026-04-30).
- **Distribution formats.** PDF for email/press kit, interactive web lookbook, print for meetings, wholesale asset; measure downloads, email CTR, inquiries, press pickups, linked sales. [Fabrikn](https://www.fabrikn.com/blog/how-to-create-a-fashion-brand-lookbook/) (2026-03-29).
- **Per-SKU e-commerce shot lists.** 6-10 core images per SKU (front, back, sides, ghost mannequin, flat lay, detail, lifestyle); D2C "deepest" list adds 2-4 detail crops, 2-3 lifestyle, 360/video; category table (T-shirt 4/6/8, shirt 5/7/10, dress 6/8/10). Vendor claims "listings with 6+ images convert 2×" without source. [Uniqs Studio](https://uniqsstudio.com/apparel-product-photography-shot-list-clothing-sku/) (2026-08-22). Separate catalog images from brand storytelling; ~60% consistent conversion-first catalog, ~40% detail/lifestyle; lock camera height, distance, light and shot order for reshoot matching. [AcquireConvert](https://acquireconvert.com/fashion-photography/clothing-photography/) (2026-04-15). 5-8 images: main on white, on-model front, on-model back/three-quarter, fabric close-up, detail-on-body, lifestyle/scale, optional variant grid or video. [Snappyit](https://snappyit.ai/blog/clothing-photoshoot-guide) (2026-05-26).
- **Lookbook timing in launch sequencing.** Planned collections: lookbook publishes Week -2 (BS Agency) or T-3 with pricing (Shanghai Garment). Streetwear: preview/lookbook 1-7 days before drop; Palace posts Thursday for Friday (Restock Blog). Broken Planet shows nothing until the site updates at drop time (Finally Offline). Drop campaigns should point to a landing page carrying "the story, the lookbook and the full lineup" (Fudge.ai).

- **Lookbook sequencing evidence is indirect.** Timing (Week -2 or T-3 for planned launches, 1-7 days for streetwear, zero pre-reveal for Broken Planet) is assembled across several sources rather than stated by one authoritative guide.
