## Meta description (149 chars)

## Body

His grandmother used to say it plain: "Love hurts, baby. That's how you know
it's real." She wasn't being poetic. She was teaching survival.

That line is where Love Hurts starts, and "Hurts" isn't a theme borrowed for a
collection name. It's the bloodline that raised him — three generations of
people who kept loving when the world didn't love them back, and never once
stopped to call it a wound instead of a survival.

They called him Beast. He'd tell you they were right.

The Beast didn't hide because he was ugly. He hid because he loved something so
beautiful it terrified him — a rose under glass, protected, not because it was
fragile for show but because it was the one thing worth keeping safe. Every
member of the Hurts family who kept loving anyway is in that image. The
collection is the glass case.

Crimson for the blood shared. Deep purple for the bruises that became wisdom.
Burgundy for the wine grandmama poured when she said, "Baby, you survived
another one." None of that palette is decoration. It's a family history you can
wear without having to explain it out loud.

This isn't a theme. It's a family name, carried the way the people who raised
him carried it — heavy, not defeated. Wear your heart. Own your scars. That was
never a slogan either.
