## Title

Luxury Runs in the Family.

## Meta description (148 chars)

The whole brand is named after her. Now she wears what he built. The story
behind Kids Capsule — the fourth chapter, and the reason for all of it.

## Body

The whole brand is named after her — Skyy Rose. His daughter.

She was on the way when he had nothing. No drive, no money, no support. But that
baby coming changed everything, and he needed to build something she'd be proud
to carry one day. Everything the brand became started as an answer to that one
need.

"I built SkyyRose so my daughter would never have to wonder if she was enough."

Kids Capsule is the brand completing its circle — the daughter who was the
reason for all of it now wears the name built around her. Same craftsmanship.
Smaller silhouettes. Passing the torch on the same terms that built the brand in
the first place, which means no shortcuts taken just because the sizing runs
smaller.

"No pastels. No cartoons. Skyy Rose doesn't wear that. She wears what her father
built — premium, dark, elegant. Scaled down but never dumbed down."

Rose gold and soft pink, but the softness lives in the color, not in the
attitude. Legacy isn't inherited passively here. It's worn, on purpose, by
someone who was the reason the whole thing got built.

This isn't nostalgia. It's inheritance — and it's not finished yet.
