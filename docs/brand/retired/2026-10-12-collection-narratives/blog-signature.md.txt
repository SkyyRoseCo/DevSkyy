## Title

Not Basics. Blueprints.

## Meta description (152 chars)

Before the drops, before the press, there was a man in Oakland sketching a
script logo at 4 AM because he couldn't afford dinner. This is Chapter One.

## Body

He drew the first rose on a night he couldn't afford dinner.

Broke, a baby on the way, every manufacturer he'd worked with had already
scammed him — and he sat there anyway, sketching a script logo until 4 AM,
because something in him knew that if he got it right, everything would change.
Signature is that night, made permanent.

This is Chapter One, not because it came first on a calendar, but because
everything else grew from it. Before the limited drops and the press features,
there was a father in Oakland who believed everyday clothes should feel like
something. Not basics. Blueprints.

"They told me luxury doesn't come from Oakland. I said  — and I meant that literally. This collection is the concrete.
Everything else grew from here."

The Bay Bridge shows up in this collection because the bridge belongs to
Oakland, not the bay. It connects the Town to Frisco — it doesn't connect Frisco
to anything. Say the bridge is Oakland's and you've said it correctly. Say "Bay
Area" like the bridge belongs to the water underneath it, and you've missed the
point Signature was built to make.

Gold on dark. The rose before the thorns. No nostalgia — this isn't a collection
that looks backward. It's the cornerstone the rest of the brand stands on, and
cornerstones don't perform confidence. They just hold weight. Everyday doesn't
mean ordinary. It never did.
