Collection 04 — Kids Capsule

Inheritance of Elegance

"Because legacy is not inherited. It is worn."

1. Core Strategy

The campaign centers on the "Reason → Absence → Proof" narrative arc. It transitions from the industrial grit of the Oakland shipyard (The Reason) to the refined luxury of the final garments (The Proof), worn by the child who inspired the brand's birth.

2. Strategic Pillars

I. The Heir's Narrative

Moving from a father's 4 AM sketches to a daughter's birthright. Legacy as an active choice, not a passive gift.

II. Precision at Scale

280gsm heavyweight cotton. Double-stitched seams. No cartoons. No pastels. Scaled down, never dumbed down.

III. The Rose Gold Thread

The literal and metaphorical binding element. Using #B76E79 to unify the Oakland soil with global luxury standards.

3. Moments of Delight

  • Digital Torch Pass: Interactive scroll animation re-enacting the founding moment.
  • Hidden Thread: Discovery-based delight in the garment seams.
  • Oakland Soil Card: A physical heirloom included with every order — unanswerable proof of origin.
