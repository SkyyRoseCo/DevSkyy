# Fit, Imagery, Reviews, and Returns

> **SKYYROSE LLC · FASHION THEME BRAIN**

## Product truth

`DURABLE` Fashion commerce must bridge what a shopper cannot touch, try on, or see
in person. Product content is operational data, not decoration. Every fact and image
must trace to the catalog or approved media registry.

## Minimum PDP information model

- Product name, SKU/identifier, collection/category and price state.
- Verified color names and color-sensitive media.
- Materials, construction, lining, stretch, weight/hand only when sourced.
- Care, origin and sustainability claims only when documented.
- Model height/measurements and worn size when authorized and accurate.
- Garment measurements, size chart units, measurement method and tolerance where available.
- Intended silhouette and fit notes in neutral language.
- Stock, preorder/backorder, delivery, duties and return/exchange eligibility.

## Media sequence

Choose sequence by the shopper's unanswered questions. A robust apparel set may
include front, back, side, detail, fabric/texture, construction, scale/on-body,
movement/video, alternate styling and packaging only when useful. Preserve color
accuracy, focal points, aspect-ratio policy, responsive sources, alt text and media
fallbacks. Do not infer image identity from filenames.

## Size and fit

- Expose sizes in the shopper's expected system while preserving canonical values.
- Group and label mixed systems; show conversion only when approved.
- Avoid forcing shoppers to self-identify with a body-category label to discover sizes.
- Separate body measurements from garment measurements.
- Explain how to measure and what ease/stretch means for this garment.
- Variant selection must update availability and any size-specific facts.
- Fit recommendations are advice, never guarantees; disclose required inputs and limitations.

## Reviews and UGC

Reviews require authenticity, moderation policy, rating distribution, sorting and
accessible controls. Structured fit feedback can include true-to-size tendency,
reviewer measurements, purchased size and optional imagery with consent. Never
generate, rewrite, suppress or selectively display reviews in a misleading way.
UGC rights and product identity must be verified.

## Returns as a product-design feedback loop

Capture reason codes at useful specificity: fit too small/large, length, cut,
color mismatch, material/quality expectation, damage, late arrival, changed mind
and product mismatch. Analyze by SKU, variant, size, imagery/content version,
channel and cohort without exposing personal data. Feed verified patterns back to
product content, size guidance, imagery and quality operations.

Do not optimize for fewer returns by hiding the policy or adding surprise friction.
Measure net value, exchange success, customer retention, support load and suitability.

## Current evidence summary

`CURRENT` Dated apparel research in `../research/fashion-commerce-research-2026-08-06.md`
supports prioritizing high-quality imagery, accurate sizing, product information,
fit-oriented reviews and transparent returns. These findings guide hypotheses; they
do not prove that a particular module or placement will lift a specific store.
