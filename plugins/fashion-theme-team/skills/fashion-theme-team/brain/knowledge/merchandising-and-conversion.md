# Merchandising and Conversion

> **SKYYROSE LLC · FASHION THEME BRAIN**

## Governing principle

`DURABLE` Merchandising reduces the distance between shopper intent and a suitable,
truthfully represented product. Conversion work removes preventable uncertainty;
it does not pressure the shopper into an unsuitable purchase.

## Discovery and navigation

- Organize navigation around the audience's language, occasions, categories, collections and verified attributes.
- Provide curated paths where visual discovery matters and search where intent is explicit.
- Keep collection names meaningful with supporting category labels; never require cultural knowledge to find basics.
- Preserve query/filter state, back-navigation position and shareable URLs where platform behavior supports it.
- Filters need counts, clear labels, selected-state visibility, reset, no-results recovery and keyboard/screen-reader support.

## Assortment presentation

Define collection thesis, hero products, breadth/depth, sorting policy, product-card
information, color aggregation, sold-out treatment, launch sequencing and content
interruptions. Editorial modules may deepen context but cannot repeatedly displace
product discovery.

Product cards should deliberately decide whether to expose price, sale state,
color count, available sizes, review summary, badges, quick add, wishlist and alternate
media. A feature is included only if its data and interaction states are real.

## Product decision zone

Above arbitrary fold rules, optimize for one coherent decision sequence:

1. Product identity and verified media.
2. Price and applicable promotion/tax context.
3. Color/material/variation selection with truthful updates.
4. Size selection, measurements and fit guidance.
5. Availability and fulfillment promise.
6. Returns/exchanges summary.
7. Primary purchase action and notices.

Then deepen evaluation with construction, fabric, care, provenance, styling,
reviews, fit feedback, shipping detail, FAQs and related products. Never bury
material purchase facts inside visual storytelling.

## Recommendations and outfits

Use catalog relationships with explicit provenance:

- complete-the-look,
- compatible layering,
- same fabrication or collection,
- alternate fit/color,
- replenishment or companion item,
- recently viewed or explicitly personalized results.

Exclude the current item, duplicates, unavailable products without a recovery path,
and incompatible size/age/category relationships. Algorithmic or inferred relations
must be labeled and measured. Track impression, interaction, add, purchase,
attachment rate and returns; protect margin, customer suitability and inventory health.

## Cart and checkout

- Preserve product/variation truth, edit paths, quantities, coupons, shipping, tax, totals and notices.
- Make destructive changes undoable where practical and announce async changes.
- Never visually suppress WooCommerce validation or payment recovery.
- Guest checkout, account creation, address entry, shipping, payment and consent must follow actual store configuration.
- Cross-sells must not destabilize totals, focus, performance or completion.

## Retention and post-purchase

Order confirmation answers what happened, what comes next, how to get help and how
to manage the order. Account, tracking, returns, exchanges, care, review requests,
replenishment and loyalty must share a consistent service promise. Loyalty value
should be immediate and understandable; enrollment and marketing consent remain separate.

## Experiment contract

Every conversion proposal that is not a durable usability or platform requirement
must include:

- hypothesis and target audience,
- primary metric,
- diagnostic metrics,
- guardrails such as returns, cancellations, accessibility, performance and margin,
- exposure unit and duration/decision rule,
- required instrumentation,
- rollback trigger.

No theme release may claim revenue or conversion uplift from a design pattern alone.
