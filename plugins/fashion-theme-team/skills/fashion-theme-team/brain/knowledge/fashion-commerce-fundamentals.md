# Fashion Commerce Fundamentals

> **SKYYROSE LLC · FASHION THEME BRAIN**  
> *SkyyRose*

## Value model

`DURABLE` A fashion storefront sells confidence in a garment, a point of view,
and a service promise. Visual desire without truthful product information creates
hesitation and returns; transactional efficiency without identity creates a
generic store. Every route must balance:

- **Desire:** recognizable world, styling possibility, material and cultural meaning.
- **Decision confidence:** fit, scale, color, construction, price, availability, delivery, returns.
- **Control:** understandable navigation, filters, comparisons, preferences, account and recovery paths.
- **Trust:** accurate content, inclusive representation, accessible interaction, secure and transparent commerce.

## Segment before designing

Record price tier, category, audience, use occasion, purchase cadence, assortment
width/depth, launch model, geography, service model, and marketplace. Do not paste
luxury conventions onto streetwear, childrenswear, basics, adaptive clothing, or
multi-brand retail.

Common strategic patterns are hypotheses, not presets:

- Luxury and high consideration: provenance, craft, service continuity, restraint, appointment/clienteling paths.
- Contemporary and premium: editorial distinction plus fast product evaluation and versatile styling.
- Streetwear and drops: cultural context, release clarity, queue/fairness, scarcity truth, mobile readiness.
- Essentials and basics: comparison, replenishment, bundles, fabric/fit clarity, repeat purchase.
- Childrenswear: caregiver decision support, age/height sizing, safety facts, durability and easy care.
- Adaptive/inclusive: functional features, body/ability representation, precise usability and fit information.
- Marketplace/multi-brand: taxonomy consistency, normalization, filters, seller/brand trust and comparison.

## Customer jobs

Map each route to one primary job and no more than two secondary jobs:

1. Discover a world or collection.
2. Locate a suitable product.
3. Judge appearance, material, use and value.
4. Determine size and fit.
5. Confirm availability, delivery, returns and total cost.
6. Complete purchase and recover from errors.
7. Track, return, exchange, care for or repurchase.
8. Build an ongoing relationship on explicit, controllable terms.

## Fashion calendar and inventory

Support evergreen, seasonal, capsule, collaboration, preorder, made-to-order,
limited drop, archive and sale models. For every model define release dates,
timezone, inventory authority, oversell behavior, backorder/preorder language,
fulfillment promise, cancellation rules and post-launch fallback. Never manufacture
scarcity or imply stock precision the data source cannot support.

## Price and promotion

Price presentation must cover regular, sale, scheduled sale, zero, unavailable,
tax-inclusive/exclusive, currency, variation ranges and installment messaging.
Promotions require eligibility, dates, exclusions, stacking behavior, accessible
terms, cart/checkout continuity and expiry recovery. A discount is not a substitute
for relevance, quality, trust or product truth.

## Ethical conversion

- No fake countdowns, fake viewers, fake stock, preselected paid extras or disguised ads.
- Do not hide shipping, duties, subscription renewal, return costs or final totals.
- Do not coerce account creation, newsletter consent, location, notifications or personalization.
- Do not use body shame, exclusionary sizing labels, manipulative defaults or inaccessible urgency.
- Preserve user choice, consent withdrawal, preference control and error recovery.

## Required strategy artifact

Return an audience/offer matrix, competitive whitespace, assortment model, route
priorities, service promise, risk register and measurement plan. Label every item
`OBSERVED`, `APPROVED`, `RECOMMENDED`, `UNKNOWN`, or `BLOCKED`; additionally label
commercial claims using the brain claim classes.
