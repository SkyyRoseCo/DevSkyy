## SkyyRose CTA system

The pack often defaults to pill buttons, liquid glass, gradient borders, or orange accents. Those are not the SkyyRose default.

- **Primary:** rose-gold `#B76E79` fill on concrete black, white label, 44px minimum target, short action verb, arrow only when it clarifies destination.
- **Secondary:** text link in white or rose gold with a hairline underline that draws on hover/focus; never the only route to a critical action.
- **Tertiary:** transparent outlined control with a concrete-surface fill on hover; use for comparison, back, or non-primary paths.
- **Commerce:** Add to Bag, Checkout, Place Order, Join Waitlist, and Submit Return remain stable, explicit, and stateful. No magnetic movement, animation delay, or visual ambiguity.
- **Motion:** one signature interaction per experience page; static on reduced motion; keyboard and touch behavior are equivalent.

## Prompt technique to use when dispatching

1. **Role + canon:** identify the specialist and load the SkyyRose artifact system, SOT policy, and relevant page blueprint.
2. **Contrastive brief:** state the desired move and the tempting generic move to reject (for example, concrete linework vs. gradient mesh).
3. **Page contract:** provide page ID, section IDs, CTA hierarchy, media provenance, states, viewports, and success criteria.
4. **Responsive transformation:** require desktop, tablet, mobile, keyboard, and reduced-motion behavior in the same prompt.
5. **Evidence return:** require `preview.html`, `contract.json`, `evidence.json`, screenshots, and explicit `PASS`, `FAIL`, or `BLOCKED` claims.
6. **Critic loop:** ask a fresh visual QA role to identify logo-off recognition, generic motion, crop failure, CTA ambiguity, and commerce truth defects.
