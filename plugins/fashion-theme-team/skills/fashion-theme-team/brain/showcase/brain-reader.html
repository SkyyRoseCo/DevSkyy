04 · Release rules

The short version.

The garment is the protagonist.

Every visual, feature, and sales decision must make the product more understandable. Never let ornamental treatment hide missing product facts, fit evidence, or service states.

SkyyRose is not a generic luxury theme.

Use Oakland-rooted context, concrete structure, monumental type, hairlines, negative space, and one rose-gold accent. Do not use generic serif luxury, gradient text, glass blur, fake scarcity, or unverified imagery.

Blueprints are not implementation claims.

The V2 plan is a planned architecture. Product media requires catalog SOT verification, editorial media requires rights clearance, and new renders require founder approval.

HTML is for eyes; JSON is for systems.

Every material handoff needs a readable rendered artifact, a strict machine contract, and candidate-bound evidence reviewed independently.
