## Why A leads

Skyy is introduced through something she does. Her response establishes awareness; her independent change establishes agency. R2 extends that agency to the purple character, making the second colorway part of the event. It has a compact action a viewer can recover without added dialogue or text.

This is a small encounter. Its eventual commercial value depends on compelling expressions, believable attention, precise timing and strong garment photography. The diagram does not prove those qualities. The 24-second equal-interval rough cut is a timing baseline, not a final pacing prescription.

The same reviewer inspected R2 without new treatment text and recovered the complete red → Skyy → purple initiative/response chain. They found the revised ending substantially clearer, while eye contact remained subtle and no deeper emotional motive or product fidelity was established. See qa/story-review.md for the distinction between the first blind read and the informed revision review.

## Main-film callback for this candidate

The proposed shared arrival begins with everyone settling independently. Purple initiates the same two-hand stance, Skyy notices and answers, and red responds. The adults retain their own character-appropriate behavior rather than all copying a children's pose. This recalls the debut's shared leadership while keeping the adult collection tones distinct. It is a proposed callback for A only and remains unapproved.
