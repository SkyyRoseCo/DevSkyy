## What this workflow changes

The unit of work is a scene with an objective and consequence. We develop the four collection arcs alongside the main film, then design images, motion and cuts to express those arcs. A shot can advance a decision, build tension, reveal something or deliberately show the product.

Spectacle remains available. A large reveal must have a setup, an identifiable subject and a consequence for how the viewer understands the scene. The workflow does not force a quiet train-home story or a mascot-guided tour.

## The working sequence

| Stage | Director's output | Check before advancing |
|---|---|---|
| Recover the brief | Fixed cast/product/source requirements, rejected ideas and open creative decisions | Current founder instructions distinguished from proposed plots |
| Develop the dramatic engine | Three different premise mechanisms, each with a visible objective and meaningful ending | Train and characters have necessary roles; removing a beat changes the story |
| Build the short/main structure | Kids debut, three adult collection arcs and a separately shaped main-commercial edit | Each short resolves its own question; main introduces Skyy early and builds a new convergence payoff |
| Rehearse the scene | Visible actions, blocking, eyelines, hand occupancy and screen direction | A fresh reviewer can follow the action; remaining uncertainty recorded |
| Design shots | Camera/lighting plan, product-readable intervals, source roles and exact excerpt placement | Proposed action fits available source material; hero and garment authority kept separate |
| Prepare images and motion | Reviewed still intent, expanded prompt, actual references and explicit canvas; then a motion brief | Prompt and reference handoff have no known conflicts; relevant creative acceptance recorded |
| Review a short sequence | Timed boards/animatic, then a bounded motion proof when authorized | Story, performance, product and continuity reviewed separately; no visual pass from prose alone |
| Produce and edit | Accepted coverage, source-preserving assembly and final review records | Technical success and founder acceptance retain separate states |

The stages reuse approvals already given. A rejection returns work to the failed layer; it does not restart every accepted decision or justify polishing rejected creative indefinitely.

## Shot card used at handoff

Read the [fillable packet](/Users/theceo/.codex/skills/skyyrose-film-directing/references/shot-packet.json) as a production record. Its empty values intentionally mean unknown, not complete.

For creative discussion, answer six questions first:

1. What does this character want right now?
2. What happens that changes the situation?
3. What does the audience learn?
4. Which exact product do we see clearly?
5. Why is the camera here, and why does it move or cut?
6. What does this shot make us need to see next?

Technical fields are filled when needed: source hashes, frame ranges, provider requests, output candidates and review receipts.
