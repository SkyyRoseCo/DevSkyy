## Revised development brief

Audience experience: initially share the man's uncertainty about a fleeting image; understand the error when Skyy looks somewhere else; finish with the satisfaction of finding the actual view together. Desired emotional impact is curiosity becoming shared discovery, not presumed nostalgia or heritage.

The protagonist wants to see the silver rose he briefly notices. A sequence of passing columns repeatedly removes its reflected image. If he keeps chasing the reflected fragment, the train carries him past the available view. Skyy actively changes the approach: she looks across the carriage, moves to that window, and waits there looking outside. He sees her cease chasing, stops his own movement, and joins her. No dialogue, presenter gesture or synchronized smile is needed.
