## Scene and reveal record

For each scene record: character objective; initial situation; initiating action; visible response; changed situation; viewer inference; exact garment interval; and the train feature necessary to the event.

For each reveal record: concealed subject; preceding clue; initial interpretation; revealing action/camera mechanism; new information; consequence; and product visibility before/after. A hero feature may support production design without being a plot mechanism; label its role honestly.

Skyy's behavior contract will specify what she notices, how she initiates, how she responds, and what she would not do. Keep behavior consistent while allowing her role to change across collections. A recurring face alone does not establish character continuity.

## Five separate review questions

| Dimension | Notes-hidden question | Recorded evidence and revision trigger |
|---|---|---|
| Story | What happened, what caused the next action, and what changed? | Verbatim response and exact confusing beat; revise action, timing or coverage if cause is missed |
| Character | What did Skyy initiate or decide? | Distinguish observed behavior from supplied backstory; revise passive or contradictory actions |
| Brand | What would need to change for another brand to use this? | Record specific world/product/behavior cues; revise a premise supported only by generic scenery |
| Product | Which garment and feature do you remember? | Compare named features with canonical references and actual visible frames; revise coverage or reject drift |
| Desire / confidence | What makes you want to learn more or buy, and what still prevents it? | Record intended adult-buyer relevance separately from creator taste; route fit/price/delivery concerns to commerce |

Do not combine these into an invented overall pass percentage. Small qualitative reviews diagnose confusion; they do not predict sales. Agent reviews are not customer studies. When Skyy moves into adult collections, explicitly ask whether her role supports adult product appeal. This is an audience question, not a reason to remove the founder's chosen character.
