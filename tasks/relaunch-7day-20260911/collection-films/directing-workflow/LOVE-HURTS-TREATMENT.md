## Meaning and perspective

Love Hurts recognizes that beauty remains in love even when it hurts. The wearer brings their own experience of happiness, pain and growth to the statement and wears it confidently. Hurts is the founder's grandmother's maiden name, but this film is not a retelling of his family history.

The film adapts Beauty and the Beast from the Beast's perspective. His emotional need is to feel loved and cared for, and to have his feelings heard. He fears losing hope that someone will hear him and return that care. This is this character's experience, not a claim about all men or a reenactment of the founder's life.

The enchanted rose expresses time slipping away. The user replaced the proposed interpersonal missed-connection opening with the rose moment. No second actor ignoring him, dialogue scene, romantic encounter or explicit rejection is required.
