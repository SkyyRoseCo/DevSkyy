# Launch research audit

Reviewed 2026-09-12. Scope: full `snapshot/research-launch.md`, including its URL ledger and gaps; four selected public-source checks. This is a review of a frozen research snapshot, not a complete validation of every linked article. No authenticated account inspection was required or performed; authentication is not applicable to these public-source checks. No campaign actions, terminal interaction with Claude, or edits to Claude's documents.

## Verdict

Useful research ledger and strong explicit uncertainty reporting. Adopt the production checklist, product-proof coverage and launch-readiness principles. Do not yet adopt its percentage budgets, return projections, email frequency, scarcity mechanics or calendar as a SkyyRose execution plan. Several operational prescriptions are accurately reported agency opinions, rather than evidence that they will improve this brand's sales.

The biggest missing translation is the latest creative decision: Kids introduces Skyy first. A commerce runbook must now connect that debut to actual Kids availability, then the other collection chapters and main film, instead of copying a generic streetwear drop.

## Decision-impact findings

### 1. Paid benchmarks omit the much smaller channel samples — material correction

Snapshot lines 37–38 can leave readers carrying the 19-store sample into paid benchmarks. The original Top Growth Marketing article specifies **Meta n=5 and Google n=3**; 19 applies to site conversion. Its ROAS is platform-attributed revenue divided by spend, using trailing account totals, not an incremental causal return from a campaign. Its numeric values are reproduced correctly, but their scope needs adding before budget decisions. [Original methodology and channel table](https://topgrowthmarketing.com/dtc-benchmarks-2026/)

Implementation: do not set SkyyRose spend against 2.70 or 4.07. Begin with verified order economics, traffic mix and measurement. The line's `1 / gross margin` formula is only a simplified merchandise-margin threshold. For a useful advertising ceiling, include payment costs, fulfillment, shipping subsidy, expected returns/cancellations and desired retained contribution. This correction is algebraic accounting reasoning; it does not establish SkyyRose's costs.

### 2. Klaviyo automation figure upgraded to direct-source support; no causal uplift established

Snapshot line 13's nearly 41% of email revenue from 5.3% of sends is directly supported by the current Klaviyo 2026 page; the public summary describes more than 183,000 customers. Replace the secondary citation for that figure with the primary report. This verifies what the platform reports, not a randomized improvement from moving campaign recipients into flows. Triggered flows and broadcasts have different recipient intent. The clothing-specific 2.15% versus 0.12% row was not independently retrieved in this check and remains secondary-supported. [Klaviyo benchmarks](https://www.klaviyo.com/products/email-marketing/benchmarks)

Implementation: prepare appropriate subscriber welcome, browse/cart recovery and post-purchase messages when technically and permission-wise appropriate. Do not promise that these will create 41% of SkyyRose revenue, nor treat a previous buyer as automatically subscribed to email or SMS.

### 3. Site conversion figures verified; Kids comparator missing

Snapshot line 37 accurately reproduces Centra's 2.4% median across 500 brands and listed vertical values. The same primary page reports **2.1% for baby/childwear**, relevant to the current first release. Its text also explains that many sampled stores are original brands attracting visitors who specifically intend to buy from them. This matters when comparing those visits with cold cinematic-ad traffic. [Centra report](https://centra.com/news/conversion-rate-fashion-ecommerce-benchmarks)

Implementation: use these as broad reference ranges, not a pass/fail threshold or sales forecast. Compare SkyyRose by device, returning versus new visitor, landing product, channel and in-stock versus pre-order state. Do not average these datasets into a new universal target.

### 4. The 3–5× drop claim is present but still anecdotal

Snapshot line 10 correctly labels the agency-experience basis. Live reading confirms Landing Partners makes the claim but does not disclose a sample, matched control, absolute sales, spend comparison or contribution results in the article. Its current header shows **21 July 2026**, so `undated` can be updated in the research ledger. [Landing Partners article](https://www.landing.partners/blog/fashion-drop-marketing-collection-launch-strategy)

Implementation: borrow anticipation and preparation; do not forecast a 3–5× return or automatically assign 30–40% to awareness. Do not shut off otherwise productive existing-product sales campaigns merely because a new capsule has not opened. Product availability and campaign purpose determine the appropriate objective.

### 5. Scarcity and social proof must follow actual conditions

Snapshot lines 7, 10, 20–28 describe countdowns, last calls, gated stores and brand-specific spectacle. The document responsibly flags unreliable Broken Planet reporting and date conflicts. These are examples of commercial execution, not evidence that removing shopping access or compressing a sales window will grow a recovering brand.

Implementation: maintain direct shopping access; use a real release date if ready. Never convert catalog edition size into units remaining. `Best sellers`, customer reactions and `last chance` messages require actual supporting sales, permissioned feedback or a real deadline. If those are absent, publish garment fit/detail content instead. Do not reproduce the agency's scheduled markdown ladder without inventory and margin justification.

### 6. Readiness and image coverage are the strongest reusable outputs

Snapshot lines 8–9, 33–36 and 45–50 contribute practical work: offer/fulfillment clarity, independent checkout checks, consistent on-model fronts/backs/details, channel compositions and an explicit retouching boundary. The Frahm reported growth and AllSaints example do not isolate a website redesign's causal impact, but the operational questions remain useful.

Implementation: separate three evidence records: accepted creative, working purchase path, and launch authorization. For br-006, carry the known pre-order requirement into PDP/cart/checkout messaging; live stock and shipping promises still need verification. Preserve original Black Rose performer and silent unchanged video excerpts. AI scenes may create the story; source-verified product evidence must establish what buyers receive.

## Kids-first implementation delta

1. Build one readiness row per actual Kids SKU: variant availability, fit and garment views, final purchase destination, delivery promise, net contribution inputs and support owner. No existing-stock assumption.
2. Finish the Kids story/reveal and identity continuity before serial reuse of Skyy. The first film must communicate product and character even to someone who never sees the later chapters.
3. Derive a short product cut, still/detail assets and alternate openings from accepted footage. Wordless film remains intact; commerce information lives in surrounding copy and shopping UI.
4. Plan the other chapters and main film together, but give each collection its own offer and destination. The previously proposed later order is a creative recommendation, not an externally proven launch sequence.
5. Decide between immediate purchase, clearly specified pre-order or permissioned notification using actual product readiness. Do not send every viewer to a waitlist simply because the drop articles do.
6. Before public release, verify source/SKU match, mobile product selection, cart/checkout, order confirmation and deduplicated purchase measurement. Existing checks or public source citations do not prove this fresh customer journey.
7. Use the seven days for release coordination, with audience response, order volume and fulfillment evidence determining changes. The cited 8–12-week production templates do not establish that a seven-day release is wrong; preparation duration and publishing cadence are different.

## Remaining research boundaries

No current SkyyRose customer cohort, consent state, inventory, channel performance, attribution settings, costs or fulfillment operations were inspected. No legal conclusions are adopted from the vendor legal summaries at line 36; any necessary implementation should use applicable primary rules. The source ledger's `used=yes` must not be read as `independently verified`. The report should keep explicit badges for direct source, secondary attribution, agency hypothesis, illustration and authenticated execution; only the first four exist here.
