## 1. Define the offer and the buyer

The current local catalog marks br-006, The Bomber Sherpa, as a pre-order, with a listed price of 95. This is source data, not a fresh live-store availability or pricing check. A purchase campaign needs confirmed price, variants, delivery expectations, cancellation/returns terms and an acceptable order margin. The catalog's edition quantity is not evidence of remaining stock or a legitimate countdown. [5]

If the pre-order commitment can be supported, present it plainly on the destination and in surrounding ad copy. If it cannot, retain Black Rose as a creative subject while directing immediate-sales spend toward a confirmed fulfillable product. A source flag of non-pre-order does not prove inventory exists. Do not invent scarcity, discounts or shipping dates to manufacture urgency.

Start with behavioral audience hypotheses:

- **Returning brand customers and engaged subscribers:** remind them of the product and what is newly available. Use actual permitted contact and behavior records, not assumed consent.
- **New adult streetwear buyers:** lead with silhouette, styling, distinctive garment details and a recognizable point of view from The Town. Validate the hypothesis against actual customer and channel evidence.
- **Adult parents and gift buyers:** develop the Kids reveal around the brand's family origin and usable product information. Do not assume that depicting children means buying advertising against children.

The brand guide is broad enough to include all ages, but the first test needs a specific purchase motivation. These are proposed groups, not discovered high-performing segments. [6]

## 3. Direct the film around desire

The audience should leave wanting to wear the garment and know which garment it is. The story supplies emotion and attention; it must allow inspection of real silhouette, texture and marks. Skyy's role should change the event, rather than repeat a smile-and-point invitation in every collection.

Develop two or three distinct Kids debut mechanisms with the founder and fashion design system engineer, using the current rough boards as evidence rather than assuming Route A is selected. Each needs a visible objective, consequence and payoff. Develop adult collection arcs alongside this work while qualifying the protected Black Rose edit separately. Review silent boards without their explanatory paragraph. The train must contribute through movement, geography, timing or sightlines. Large reveals should have an earlier clue and a consequence.

Plan three opening hypotheses from the accepted story:

1. **Product recognition:** a compelling, readable view of the featured Kids garment appears immediately; the next cut expands its context. Black Rose later applies this principle to actual bomber footage.
2. **Action in progress:** the model is already doing something purposeful; Skyy's response introduces the unanswered question.
3. **Withheld context:** an identifiable detail precedes a reveal that changes its meaning, without concealing the clothing for the entire ad.

These are categories for boards and tests, not completed scenes. Hold the approved middle and ending constant when testing openings. If an opening changes the plot or audience promise, treat it as a different concept rather than a clean hook comparison.

TikTok's creative guidance supports a hook/body/close structure and consistent vertical framing. Its older fashion guidance also describes on-model product detail and styling, while explicitly labeling its patterns as observations rather than compulsory formulas. Adapt those principles to SkyyRose's wordless direction; do not copy unrelated trends, fake testimonials or bargain-led imagery. [7][8]

## 5. Prepare the purchase path

A br-006 ad should land on br-006 or a compact shopping page where it is immediately visible. The same garment, color and construction must connect the film with the destination. Show front/back, relevant detail, size selection, fit guidance, exact price, honest availability and shipping expectations, plus a clear purchase path. The main commercial can land on the collection index, but shoppers need direct access to the featured products.

Verify the mobile journey before paid traffic: product page, size selection, cart, checkout, successful/failed payment behavior in the appropriate test environment and order confirmation. The existing immersive site can provide depth, while preserving direct shopping routes. An immersive sequence should not become a mandatory obstacle between product interest and checkout.

Track product view, add-to-cart, checkout, purchase and refund using stable SKU and creative identifiers. Reconcile purchases with actual store orders. GA4 ecommerce requires implementation; unique transaction IDs prevent repeated purchase events from inflating web-stream counts. Do not equate a purchase-button click with an order. [9]

Use the first seven days as a release sequence, not a statistical deadline. Complete the required creative and commerce checks before announcing a fixed launch date. If an asset fails, adjust the schedule instead of spending to maintain a calendar.

| 6 | Main commercial combining the established stories | Connect the collection worlds and expose clear shopping paths |
| 7 | Best-supported creative and product follow-up | Review valid orders, attribution lag, feedback and the next targeted revision |

Distribution should follow existing customer/channel evidence. Instagram/Reels is a candidate for fashion discovery; TikTok is another candidate, not an automatic simultaneous paid launch. Concentrate paid learning on one suitable channel initially if resources are limited. Check current account capabilities before specifying the final campaign setup.

TikTok distinguishes Sales from Video Views, and its Sales documentation describes website destinations with phased feature availability. Use an objective aligned with buying when purchase is the outcome. For WooCommerce, do not assume a TikTok Shop or GMV Max workflow. Its split-testing documentation supports separate audience groups for controlled creative comparison. Two ads receiving unequal algorithmic delivery are not automatically a controlled test. [10]

For email, start with reachable subscribed audiences, consider recent clicks/site behavior and retain suppression state. Past purchase alone does not establish marketing consent. Klaviyo cautions that privacy-protected opens can inflate engagement signals; do not define the entire audience from opens alone. Existing customers may be valuable, but list size, quality and purchasing response remain unverified. [11]

## 7. Budget and decision rules

Calculate allowable acquisition cost from the order economics before selecting a media budget. A first-order ceiling can start from realized net revenue less product cost, payment fees, fulfillment, shipping subsidy and expected return/cancellation costs. Choose the contribution to retain after acquisition. This is a planning calculation; actual costs and return rates are still needed. Do not justify losses with an unmeasured lifetime-value assumption.

Pre-order cash received is not the same as fulfilled contribution. Track commitments, cancellations and eventual fulfillment alongside paid acquisition.

| Observed pattern | Investigation |
|---|---|
| Weak early attention | Opening composition, clarity, pacing or audience relevance |
| Good viewing, weak product visits | Product recognition, curiosity without buying intent, CTA or click destination |
| Good visits, few carts | Fit, price/value, availability, product-page consistency |
| Carts, few paid orders | Shipping cost/timing, payment friction, trust or checkout defects |
| Orders with weak contribution | Acquisition cost, product economics, cancellations/returns |

These are diagnostic hypotheses, not automatic causal conclusions. Track platform delivery, attributed sales, store orders and contribution separately. Do not sum competing platforms' claimed conversions into unique revenue. Attribution credit alone does not prove incremental purchases.

Scale when accepted creative, a functioning purchase journey and sufficient economic evidence agree. Keep testing contained when volume is too low to distinguish signal from noise. A seven-day release schedule does not guarantee a winning ad or regained traction.
