## Direction

Ground the film in the physical presence of the actual Black Rose footage: a believable person, a real garment, restrained behavior and specific light. Use the train as a lived-in environment. Let character action determine when the camera moves and when an edit happens.

Working dramatic premise for development: a man is trying to make it home on the last train. The film follows boarding, passage and arrival. Skyy travels alongside him. The adults encountered along the route have a physical role in his passage; they do not line up to display outfits. The final arrival reveals the children he has been travelling toward. This is a proposed fictional campaign scene, not a claim about the source performer's actual family.

The story must establish the destination or person he is seeking through a visible, nonverbal clue in its first seconds. That clue is unresolved; do not render a journey whose purpose becomes legible only after a written explanation.

## Visual rules for the new treatment

- No giant floating roses, obvious collection monuments, posed thrones, synchronized smiles or presenting garments with open palms.
- Skyy keeps the supplied illustrated design. Performance is restrained: observing, walking, pausing and responding to another character; no host gestures or magical guidance.
- Each shot changes the situation: a missed doorway, a door held, a passage made possible, an arrival. Do not use near-identical posed A/B shots to fill a duration.
- Show garments through motivated action: a jacket front as its wearer boards, a hand on a rail, cloth moving in a turn, a brief physical-product detail. Keep source construction and marks exact.
- Collection hero references become practical setting details. Source-confirmed bridge views, architectural shapes and metalwork may inform composition; do not assume a giant symbol communicates a story.
- The Kids reveal changes the meaning of the journey. Stage it as an encounter with people, without a throne or a presentation to camera.
- All original video excerpts remain visually unchanged and silent. Edit around them. No generated replacement of source frames.
- Keep wardrobe and casting source references separate from rejected campaign imagery. No rejected frame may seed the next generation.

Develop the dramatic clue and a concise shot sequence, then make one representative scene reviewable. It must communicate an objective, action and consequence without captions. Evaluate behavior, emotional credibility, product fidelity and cinematic editing alongside character continuity. A provider completion or a contact-sheet match cannot establish story success.
