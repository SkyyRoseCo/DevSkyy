1. Direction — Veritas composition × SkyyRose identity

  1. One dominant focal point per viewport. Full-bleed imagery is the structure of every page, not decoration. Nothing competes with the opener: no filmstrips, giant emblems, numbered rails and mascot all inside one hero.
  2. Editorial sequence. Every route is Arrival → Chapters → Colophon. A chapter = a full-bleed scene (16:9 desktop, 3:2 mobile) followed by a copy/commerce band on the void. Alternate band column order for rhythm; never stack centred titles over everything.
  3. Restrained navigation. Transparent header over cinematic arrivals, solid on scroll. Small wordmark, Hanken links, one primary action per view plus one quiet text link.
  4. Garment is the protagonist. Product imagery is shown large and unframed on #0A0A0A/#111 tiles. No ornate portal frames in grids (card arg 'frame' => false; the frame system stays available for the archive test and founder-directed uses).
  5. Oakland identity, not European luxury. References: Kith, Oaklandish, Culture Kings, Fear of God, Palm Angels. Copy comes from skyyrose2_collections() and founder text only; never invent product facts (registry via get_product).
  6. Motion is inherited. Reuse the existing controllers and their DOM hooks (§6). Reduced motion always yields the static composition. No new JS unless the contract names it.
