## BLACK ROSE — getting ready

I want you to feel proud of who you are.

Black is beautiful.

And I want you to feel that when you put this on.

Wear it how you wear it.  
Bring your own style to it.

I want you to look in the mirror…  
and feel good about who you see.

[Pause. Let the look between friends land before the scene changes.]

## SIGNATURE — making our own image

Signature takes me back to where it all started.

Oakland is part of that.  
It’s part of me.

And when you put these pieces on,  
I want you to bring your own story with you.

The way you wear it.  
The way you carry yourself.

I made the piece…  
but I want to see what you do with it.

[Pause. The photographer hands over the camera and steps into the frame.]

## LOVE HURTS — letting someone closer

With Love Hurts,  
I kept thinking about the Beast’s side of the story.

Wanting somebody close…  
and being scared to let them get that close.

You can care about somebody  
and still take a little time to let them in.

Sometimes it starts small.

You move your bag.  
You make a little room beside you.

And you let them sit.

[Stop speaking. Hold on the two people sitting together as the protagonist’s hand relaxes.]
