## Final interpretation and additional state coverage

The final inventory verdict is NOT CLEAR: 13 distinct URLs have recorded automated findings; 67 URLs have no automated violations in the tested states. Five rule groups remain: color contrast, unnamed links, duplicate landmark names, heading order, and content outside landmarks. No critical-impact axe violations were reported. Best-practice rules (landmark names, heading order, regions) are distinguished from directly tagged WCAG failures (contrast and link names); this is not a claim that all findings are separate WCAG failures.

- High priority: `/worlds/love-hurts/` uses small crimson text measured at 3.96:1 against #0a0a0a, below the rule's 4.5:1 threshold. Use a compliant text color while preserving the stronger crimson for suitable decorative treatments.
- High priority: `/category/collections/` and `/category/collections/signature-collection/` render an empty tabbable `.sr2-journal-card__media` anchor without an accessible name. Supply the article's accessible name or omit the empty redundant media link.
- Moderate: seven lookbook/world/collection URLs reuse landmark names; incorporate each scene or collection name into the accessible label.
- Moderate: `/worlds/`, `/track-order/`, `/wishlist/` jump to Jetpack's `h3` sharing title; correct the hierarchy or use non-heading markup for that utility label.
- Moderate: `/step-inside-the-signature-virtual-experience/` has its reply heading outside a landmark.
- Manual semantics: homepage film wrapper uses `aria-label` on a generic div. Add a suitable semantic grouping role if the group needs an accessible name. This was reported as axe incomplete, not as a confirmed automatic violation.

The initial mobile `/product/kids-001/` scan flagged 14 footer targets at 18px height. Two targeted reruns and a full-rule rerun found no target-size violations. Preserve this as an intermittent observation requiring delivery/state follow-up, not a currently reproduced stable defect. The first mobile search scan received HTTP429 and audited the host's error response; its contrast/landmark/overflow findings are NOT attributed to the search template. A subsequent normal HTTP200 mobile search scan passed. Original evidence is retained in `all-pages-initial.json`; the final matrix uses the explicit successful rechecks. No original failure evidence was erased.
